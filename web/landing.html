FAQ

Do I need my own API keys?

Yes — you paste keys for the providers you want (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, and the rest). Requests go from your server straight to the provider, so you pay the provider directly for what you use. There is no middleman markup.

Where are my keys stored?

On your server, in your database. The UI only ever shows them masked, and they are used solely to call the provider you connected them for. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Can I use local models?

Yes. Point Omniilink at a local Ollama server and its models show up next to the cloud ones. Any OpenAI-compatible local endpoint (vLLM, LM Studio, and similar) works through the custom provider form.

What does auto-route actually do?

Each request is scored against the models you have connected and sent to the cheapest one that can handle the job — long documents go to long-context models, code to code-strong ones. You can pin any model manually per message or globally.

Is it really free?

The app is free while in beta, including every feature listed above. Your own provider usage is billed by your providers at their rates. A paid Pro tier is coming soon for people who want a hosted option.

Does it work offline?

With local Ollama models, yes — chat, coding, and games all run without internet once the app is installed. Cloud providers need their usual connectivity.
